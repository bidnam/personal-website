About

The most important strategic decisions aren't made in boardrooms—they're made in the moments where complexity meets clarity.

I'm a strategist who helps companies figure out who they need to be to win. My work spans brand strategy, business transformation, and the systems that connect the two.

I started as a writer—the kind who believed that if you could find the right words, you could move people to act. An MBA sharpened that instinct with analytical rigor. A decade in consulting taught me that winning isn't just about the right message or the right strategy. It's about building the systems that make both executable.

That belief has taken me across B2B technology, consumer goods, healthcare, retail, and financial services. I've led brand positioning for Fortune 100 enterprises, developed value creation strategies for PE-backed portfolios, and built marketing transformation programs from the ground up. The contexts vary. The approach stays consistent: ask the right questions, engage the right people, find the right solutions together.

Before consulting, I coached high school lacrosse—transforming a program that had never had a winning season into state champions. The lesson stuck: strategy without shared belief is just a document. Belief without strategy is just hope.

I'm currently a Senior Partner at Ogilvy Consulting, where I lead brand and business transformation for enterprise clients. I was employee #2 at COLLINS when it launched its strategy practice, helping build the team that would be named AdAge's Agency of the Year.
